Background

Data professional with 4+ years of industry experience in ad tech and marketing analytics, complemented by multidisciplinary graduate training in Data Science, Computer Information Sciences, and Innovation Management. My work in NLP and sentiment analysis has deepened my interest in how language and culture intersect — and how AI systems reflect the social contexts that produce them. I am pursuing doctoral research to investigate these questions rigorously.

Research Interests

I study how social and cultural forces shape large language models, with a focus on social media as a lens — how online communities, platform norms, and cultural context influence both the data that trains AI systems and the outputs they produce. My background in NLP and sentiment analysis grounds this work in computational methods, while my broader interest lies in understanding AI as a cultural artifact shaped by the societies that build it.

YouTube Comment Sentiment & Sponsor Detection
2026
Designed and implemented an end-to-end data pipeline to collect, preprocess, and analyze audience sentiment toward sponsored YouTube content.
